## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

Toxic Noir & Bioluminescent Glamour - A shadowy, vevlet-draped underground VIP club reclaimed by glowing, hypnotic flora where beauty hides deadly venom.

**Storyline:**

You are a private investigator who sneaks into *The Velvet Nightshade*, an exclusive subterranean lounge beneath the city, after several high-profile guests vanish following a private gala. Inside, you discover that the club's enigmatic owner has laced the ventilation system with hypnotic orchid pheromones to trap and control everyone inside. To break the pheromone trance, gather evidence, and confront the mastermind without falling under her spell, you must explore six opulent rooms across the club to collect protective gear and counter-agents before entering the Emerald VIP Vault.

## Rooms

2. Sunken Bar
3. Bioluminescent Terrace
4. Dressing Room
5. Perfume Atelier
6. Champagne Cellar
7. Security Booth
8. Emerald VIP Vault (Villain room)


## Items

1. Silver Flask (Sunken Bar)
2. UV Flashlight (Bioluminescent Terrace)   
3. Silk Scarf (Dressing Room)
4. Neutralizing Elixir (Perfume Atelier)
5. Master Keycard (Champagne Cellar)
6. Encrypted Ledger (Security Booth)

## Villain

Lilith Belladonna - A charismatic, ruthless perfumer and club owner who distills rare, mind-altering toxins from nocturnal flowers and commands lethal thorny vines from inside the Emerald VIP Vault.
